SCENE 01 THE CUP
1997

THE PHILIPPINES

A new coffeehouse story arrives in Makati.

Starbucks opened its first Philippine store at 6750 Ayala Building in Makati on December 4, 1997. The Philippines became the company’s third market outside North America.

The fit was natural: coffee, warm hospitality and a culture that values conversation and connection.

SCENE 02 MAKATI
1971

THE BEGINNING

Before the coffeehouse, there was a small store in Seattle.

Starbucks began in 1971 at Pike Place Market, selling freshly roasted whole-bean coffees. The name drew from the seafaring world of Moby-Dick, echoing the journeys of early coffee traders.

One storefront. A passion for coffee. A story still unfolding.

SCENE 03 SEATTLE

THE COFFEEHOUSE IDEA

Coffee becomes a place, not just a drink.

After experiencing Italian coffee bars in 1983, Howard Schultz imagined a coffeehouse centered on conversation, belonging and community — a “third place” between home and work.
